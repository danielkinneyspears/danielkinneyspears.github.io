Privacy Choices

Nothing here is a scavenger hunt. These are the controls, what each one changes, and what Booka cannot yet do.

BookaPrivacyChoicesTermsSupportAI & content

Effective September 1, 2026 · Last updated September 1, 2026 · Version 0.9 · internal beta draft

The current beta does not have accounts or a cloud Library. Most personal story and route data is on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes its local container; use in-app deletion first when you want to keep the app.
TestFlight 1.0 (5) is available to the one-person internal group and includes the controls below. The build has not yet been installed or field-tested, and external distribution remains disabled.

01Location

  1. Open Booka → Settings → Location.
  2. Open iPhone Settings and choose the location access you want.
  3. Turning it off prevents new live walking routes; it does not upload or erase your saved route.

02Story, voice, and cover processing

Booka asks separately before first use of cloud story generation, ElevenLabs narration, and generated cover art. You can withdraw those permissions in Booka Settings. Withdrawing stops future requests; it does not recall work already processed by a provider.

  • Declining custom-story processing prevents new custom AI stories.
  • Declining cloud narration uses an available on-device voice or stops that preparation honestly.
  • Declining generated covers keeps the Booka Ducka template and leaves every story function available.

03Daily comparisons

Turn Contribute my Daily choices on or off in Settings. Off means your future semantic choice path is not submitted. You can still view aggregate percentages when the service has at least one recorded path; the current beta does not enforce a minimum privacy threshold. Submitted aggregate contributions cannot be separated later because they contain no account identity.

04Optional product analytics

Analytics is off by default. Turning it off stops future optional events and deletes the local installation identifier. The current export intentionally omits that identifier, and Booka has no account linking prior events to you, so dks.studio may be unable to locate already-submitted raw events. Raw events expire after about 30 days.

05Delete or export local data

  • One story: Library → story details → Delete Story. This removes its local record, route, choices, audio, and cover, but not prior server/provider records or its locally saved scene-feedback event.
  • Everything local: Settings → Delete All Local Data.
  • Export: Settings → Export story history. The JSON omits precise routes, audio, cover files, checkpoint, and analytics identifier.
  • Unfinished story: Settings → Remove unfinished quest.

If a control is absent from an older internal TestFlight build, update to the current test build or delete the app to remove its local container.

06Ask for help

The intended address is hello@dks.studio, but mailbox delivery has not yet been verified. Once verified, use “Booka privacy” in the subject. Do not include a route screenshot, home address, story transcript, or API key. State the app version, the control you used, and what result you expected. Read the full privacy policy.
