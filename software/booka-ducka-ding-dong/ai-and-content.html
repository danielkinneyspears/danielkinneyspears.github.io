04How generated stories and covers work

The app sends the complete custom setup to the dks.studio backend, including genre, mood, prose style, intensity, audience, perspective, duration, narrator alias, route shape, control mode, and optional premise. The backend sends only genre, mood, prose style, intensity, audience, perspective, duration, and premise through Vercel AI Gateway to OpenAI. It does not send route coordinates.

Current position, walked trace, route revisions, learned walking speed, and per-walk pace measurements are not sent to OpenAI or ElevenLabs. Adaptive routing is handled through Apple maps and directions plus on-device app state.

The story model returns a structured graph and five-field cover brief. After the graph passes validation, the backend signs that brief. If generated-cover permission is on, the app returns the signed token and the image model receives only setting, focal subject, symbolic object, atmosphere, and palette. It does not receive route geometry, identity, complete prose, choices, or endings. A premise detail may be reflected in the brief, so do not put personal information in the optional premise.

The reviewed release-candidate cover configuration is OpenAI GPT Image 2 at medium quality in a native portrait size. The app receives a provider-backed manifest and labels the result AI-generated; a failure or declined permission uses the deterministic Booka template.

Restricted story and narration receipts record a narrower technical set such as model or voice, usage, latency, request identifiers, cache result, bytes, and shadow cost. Cover manifests and receipts additionally record provider/model, quality, policy versions, image dimensions, file hash, warnings, and spoiler-safe alt text. Generated-cover provider/model details are also shown in the local completed-story view.

05Review, rights, and reports

The current backend checks story graph structure and fully decodes cover files before checking type, size, dimensions, and hash. Prompt rules discourage brands, real people, named-artist imitation, unsafe addresses, text artifacts, and spoilers. OpenAI applies its provider filtering, but Booka does not yet perform substantive story or image moderation, originality detection, copyright or brand detection, or spoiler detection. In-app content reporting is not yet available.

Public creator submissions remain disabled until rights attestations, moderation, reporting, blocking, takedown, and appeal systems are operational.
