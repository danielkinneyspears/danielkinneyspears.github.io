Booka Ducka Ding Dong

You walk while Booka paces a branching audiobook against route progress. At a decision, you choose with buttons or—in Movement beta—through a sustained change in walking direction. Loop mode aims to bring the story and route back near the start; timing, routing, and Movement inference remain beta features.

Booka Ducka Ding Dong turns a real walk into a branching audiobook. It plans a return-to-start route, paces scenes against progress, asks meaningful left-or-right questions, remembers the consequences, and saves the finished story and walked route for replay.

01What you can test

  • A free Featured Quest targeting about 40 minutes and defaulting to a return-to-start loop. Finished ElevenLabs duration and real-world route timing are still being validated.
  • Custom AI-generated stories targeting 15–60 minutes. The selected duration is a generation and routing target, not a guaranteed finished runtime.
  • Buttons or Movement beta for choices, with Buttons always available as the manual authority.
  • A persistent audiobook player, lock-screen controls, local Library, replay, and offline audio.
  • Separate story, narration, and cover-art provenance in the source candidate’s completed-story details.

02How the AI is labeled

Creation facts are independent. A story can be human-authored and synthetically narrated; an AI-generated story can use human-created cover art.

03The honest route caveat

Booka is an entertainment app, not navigation or a safety service. Movement beta detects a sustained change in walking direction; it does not prove that a particular street is a legal or safe turn. Follow laws, signs, conditions, and your own judgment. Stop and use Buttons whenever looking at or handling the phone would be unsafe.
