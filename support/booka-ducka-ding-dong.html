Booka is entertainment, not turn-by-turn navigation. Do not follow a story choice into traffic, a closed path, private property, unsafe terrain, or anywhere local signs and judgment say not to go. Buttons remain the manual fallback.

01Fast fixes

  • No route: verify Location is allowed While Using the App, move outdoors, wait for a better location fix, and try a shorter route.
  • Wrong position or rerouting: pause somewhere safe, use recenter, and wait for Booka to reacquire the route. Do not make a story choice during recovery.
  • No voice: check the in-app narration status and your network. Booka may offer the system voice when ElevenLabs is unavailable.
  • Offline replay is silent: turn on cloud narration permission to create or restore an ElevenLabs download, open the saved story on a connection, and download it again. Booka should never display a no-op Play button.
  • Old story resumed: Settings can remove the unfinished checkpoint without deleting completed Library stories.

02Send a useful report

The intended support address is hello@dks.studio, but mailbox delivery has not yet been verified. External distribution remains gated on a working contact. Once verified, use “Booka support” in the subject and include:

  • App version/build and iPhone/iOS version.
  • Whether it was Featured, archived, or custom; loop or destination; Buttons or Movement.
  • What you expected, what happened, and whether retrying changed it.
  • The exported story-history JSON, if it is relevant and you deliberately choose to share it.

Do not send your home address, precise route, full story transcript, provider key, or unrelated personal information.

03Privacy and content

Use Privacy Choices for permission, deletion, and export help. An in-app content-report action is not available yet. Once the support mailbox is verified, email the story title or revision and category without pasting the whole work.

04Current beta limits

The beta does not promise route-native intersection binding, flawless Movement inference, multi-hour stories, accounts, cross-device Library sync, billing, a new quest every day, or public creator publishing. Those features stay out of public claims until their tests pass.
