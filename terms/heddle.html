02What Heddle does

You name a subject and a specific thing you want to make. Heddle designs a dated course working backward from that thing, breaks it into daily sessions, and has a tutor read what you submit before writing what comes next. Courses and tutor readings are generated by AI and can differ between runs.

03Subscriptions

Heddle Plus is an auto-renewable monthly or annual subscription. App Store prices are shown before purchase and vary by region.

  • Apple charges your account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The subscription renews unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Apple charges renewal within 24 hours before the current period ends.
  • Manage or cancel it in Apple Account settings. Deleting Heddle does not cancel it.
  • Buying a subscription forfeits any unused portion of an offered free trial.

Apple handles purchases; I never see payment details. Ask Apple for refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

04Credits

One standard tutor reading costs one credit; a deep reading costs five.

  • Monthly allowance: granted with the plan, resets monthly, and does not roll over.
  • Purchased credits: stay in a separate non-expiring balance; the allowance is spent first.

Credits meter Heddle use. They are not currency or property, have no cash value, and cannot be transferred or redeemed. Purchased packs are consumable in-app purchases and are non-refundable except where Apple or law requires otherwise. A single-course purchase unlocks one custom course and is not a subscription.

06Photographs

A submitted photo is sent to the tutoring service to be read and is not published. Location and camera metadata are stripped before it leaves the device. Photograph your own work; do not submit photos of people without their agreement. The privacy page covers website and product links.

07What the tutor is not

The tutor is a knowledgeable reader, not a licensed professional. It can be confidently wrong and reads only your photograph and description. Nothing it produces is medical, legal, financial, nutritional, safety, or other professional advice. Verify food safety and allergens yourself; follow manufacturers and qualified safety guidance around tools, heat, electricity, or chemicals. Do not rely on Heddle where being wrong would hurt someone.

08Guilds and what you share

A guild is up to six people making the same thing on the same dates. Joining one is optional. Inside a guild, the day’s photo or writing you choose to post, the process you logged, and the one-line reads you leave are visible to the other members of that guild and to no one else.

  • Zero tolerance.No harassment, threats, hate, sexual content, content involving minors, or anything that exposes another person’s private information. Content like that is removed, and anyone who posts it is removed from their guild and may lose access to guilds entirely.
  • Screening and reports. Posts, reads, and names are checked automatically before other members see them. Anyone can report a post or read or block another member from inside the app. I review reports and act on them, usually within 24 hours.
  • Your rights stay yours.Posting to a guild gives me only the permission needed to store your post and show it to the members of that guild. Delete a post, or leave the guild, and it’s gone.
  • Reporting a problem: use the report option in the app, or write to hello@dks.studio.
