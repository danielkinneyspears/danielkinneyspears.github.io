02What Booka is—and is not

Booka is an interactive fiction and audiobook service that uses your walking progress to pace a story. It is not a navigation, emergency, accessibility-navigation, medical, fitness, or safety service. Routes, ETA, position, and Movement inference can be delayed, incomplete, or wrong. Never rely on Booka where an error could injure someone or cause property loss.

03Walking safely

  • Follow laws, signs, closures, property boundaries, weather, lighting, accessibility needs, and real-world conditions over the app.
  • Do not look at or handle the phone while crossing, driving, cycling, or whenever doing so is unsafe.
  • A story’s “turn left” or “turn right” is a fictional choice cue. It is never permission or proof that a physical path is legal or safe.
  • A directional branch is not a promise that a matching walking route exists. Automatic recovery may use a direct route to the unchanged finish.
  • Use Buttons, pause, wait for automatic route recovery or replacement, abandon the walk, or call emergency services whenever circumstances require it.
  • You are responsible for choosing an appropriate location, route, pace, footwear, visibility, supervision, and device/audio setup.

It omits the full story, precise route geometry, raw location timestamps, accuracy and speed samples, audio, and artwork. A local download grants personal in-app listening; it does not grant a right to redistribute the story, voice, or artwork.
