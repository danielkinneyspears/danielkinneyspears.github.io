2. What Heddle does

You name a subject and a specific thing you want to make. Heddle designs a dated course working backward from that thing and breaks it into daily sessions. Each day you do the work and submit it — text, a photograph, or both. A tutor reads what you actually submitted and writes what comes next.

The courses and the tutor’s readings are generated by AI. They are produced fresh for you and will not be identical to anyone else’s, or to what you would get running the same request twice.

Do not rely on Heddle where being wrong would hurt someone.
