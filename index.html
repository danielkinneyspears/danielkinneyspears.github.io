I like small products that know what they refuse to do. Most of these started as a complaint I couldn’t drop, and got to something running as fast as I could get there. I’d rather show you a build than describe one.

The day job is frwrd.team — a proposal shop that runs federal bids for companies too small to staff one. That is a service, with people, and it lives on its own site. Everything on this one is mine.
